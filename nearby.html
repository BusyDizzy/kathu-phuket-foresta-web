🌿 Explore Nearby ​

Bang Wad Reservoir is more than just a place to walk.

It’s a small ecosystem of trails, viewpoints, quiet corners, and local life.

Take your time — there’s more to discover beyond each QR code.

🧭 Around the Reservoir ​

🚶 Walking & Running Trail ​

A full loop around the reservoir stretches for several kilometers.

  • smooth paved sections
  • shaded forest paths
  • open dam views

💡 Best time: early morning or sunset

🌄 Sunset & Dam View ​

One of the most popular spots.

  • wide open view over the water
  • soft light reflections
  • calm atmosphere

📸 This is where many of the best photos are taken

🌿 Quiet Corners ​

Step away from the main path and you’ll find:

  • hidden benches
  • small clearings
  • shaded areas under trees

💡 Perfect for reading, resting, or just doing nothing

🛕 Nearby Landmarks ​

🛕 Local Temple Area ​

A short ride or walk away:

  • peaceful surroundings
  • traditional Thai architecture
  • less crowded than city temples

⛰️ Hills & Forest Roads ​

There are small roads and trails leading up into the hills.

  • light hiking possible
  • natural viewpoints
  • more secluded atmosphere

⚠️ Go during daylight and stay on visible paths

🥥 Local Experience ​

Around the reservoir, you may find:

  • fresh coconut water
  • simple street food
  • small coffee setups

Nothing formal — just part of everyday life here.
