🌳 Dolichandrone serrulata ​

Family: Bignoniaceae

About this tree ​

Dolichandrone serrulata is the tree species marked on several original Kathu Phuket Foresta QR signs around Bang Wad Square.

This species appears across multiple points in the square.

Uses shown on the original sign ​

  • 🌿 Traditional medicine
  • 🍽 Food-related use
  • ♻️ Utility / environmental value
  • ☀️ Cultural meaning

Where to find this tree on the square ​

  • Point 1 — Bang Wad Square
  • Point 2 — Sunset Side
  • Point 3 — Pavilion Moments
  • Point 4 — Nature Corner
  • Point 5 — Hidden Crossing
